FAQ

Frequently Asked

Can I take bookings directly from the site? +
Yes. I can integrate a booking calendar (like Calendly or Acuity), a custom form, or a quote calculator — whatever fits your workflow. Some integrations have a small monthly cost.
How do I show up in local Google searches? +
I set up your Google Business Profile, add location schema markup, create service-area pages, and optimise your titles and meta descriptions for local keywords.
Do you do before/after photo galleries? +
Absolutely — they're one of the best conversion tools for cleaning companies. I'll design a polished before/after gallery. You just supply the photos.
What platform will my site be built on? +
Typically WordPress for full flexibility and easy self-editing. For smaller budgets I also build on Webflow. I'll recommend the best fit for your needs.
