FAQ

Common Questions

Can members sign up and pay through the site? +
Yes. I can integrate Stripe, GoCardless, or your existing gym management software (Mindbody, Glofox, etc.) to handle payments and memberships directly.
How does the class schedule work? +
It depends on your setup. I can embed a live schedule from your booking software (Mindbody, Acuity, Pike13), or build a simple static timetable you update yourself.
Can I update the site myself? +
Yes. All WordPress and Webflow sites include a simple dashboard for everyday updates. I provide a walkthrough video so your team can make changes without any technical knowledge.
Will you help us rank on Google? +
Every site includes on-page SEO, correct meta data, local schema, and Google Business Profile setup. Ranking timelines vary, but most clients see improvement within 30–60 days.
